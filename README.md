# Phishing Email Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze sample safe phishing emails and identify suspicious indicators. The analysis focuses on sender addresses, links, urgency, attachments, and other suspicious email characteristics.

## Phishing Indicators

The following indicators were considered during the analysis:

- Suspicious sender addresses or domains
- Misleading or suspicious links
- Urgent or threatening language
- Unexpected attachments
- Requests for sensitive information
- Spelling, grammar, or branding inconsistencies
- Unusual or unexpected requests

## Interview Questions

### How do you identify phishing?

Phishing can be identified by checking the sender's email address, suspicious links, unusual requests, urgency, unexpected attachments, and requests for sensitive information.

### What is spear phishing?

Spear phishing is a targeted phishing attack directed at a specific person or organization. It often uses personalized information to make the message appear trustworthy.

### Why are shortened links risky?

Shortened links can hide the actual destination URL, making it difficult to verify where the link will lead. They can therefore increase the risk of being redirected to a malicious or fraudulent website.

## Outcome
